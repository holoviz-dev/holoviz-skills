# Outreach

This skill covers writing about and showing HoloViz work for an outside audience. Draft and review every post, and the on-screen copy of every video, with the [`deslop` skill](../deslop/SKILL.md) as well, since a post about engineering work loses readers fastest when it reads as generated.

## References

- [Writing a Blog Post](writing-a-blog-post.md): choosing the reader and the point, structure, voice, code, figures, people and sources, and the review loop.
- [Making a Demo Video](making-a-demo-video.md): demo or blog teaser, beat sheet before the file, labeled screens with no garden paths, pacing and attention, facts only, readable style, the deterministic `window.seek(t)` HTML contract, recording to MP4 with `record_video.py`, and handling revisions.
- [Adding Voice and Music](adding-voice-and-music.md): narration with one designed or saved voice per speaker, letting the narration set the timeline, writing lines that flow and picking steady takes, captions as a soft track, sidecar or burned in, the lo-fi jazz and cinematic music presets, sound effects, and ducking and loudness.
- [Making an Explanatory Video](making-an-explanatory-video.md): a codebase walkthrough for new contributors, built on the demo's HTML contract and export: one layer and five boxes, every arrow checked against the source, one request traced end to end on a fixed stage, progressive disclosure, and an ending that shows where new code goes.
